The FMS Diagnostic Method Sequence 01–06 · © Martin Pérez

Six steps, in order, on every call.

The sequence exists so a diagnosis never depends on habit or guesswork. Each step has to clear before the next one starts — which is why the gauges come out fourth, not first.

Power→ Airflow→ Electrical→ Refrigerant→ Controls→ Environment
Step 01

Power Verification

Supply voltage, disconnects, and breakers confirmed before anything else is touched. A surprising share of "dead" systems fail right here.

Step 02

Airflow Assessment

Filters, blower, static pressure, and duct condition. Restricted airflow imitates a dozen more expensive problems — so it gets ruled out early.

Step 03

Electrical Systems

Capacitors, contactors, relays, and motor windings tested against manufacturer spec — measured with a meter, not judged by eye.

Step 04

Refrigerant Analysis

Only now do gauges go on. Superheat and subcooling are read against factory targets, so the charge is measured — never assumed.

Step 05

Controls Verification

Thermostat calibration, board logic, and safety switches walked end to end, confirming the system's brain matches its body.

Step 06

Environmental Factors

Drainage, insulation, ventilation, and heat load around the equipment — the conditions that decide whether a repair actually holds.

The Company Houston, Texas · Est. 2025

Built on a field tech's frustrations.

Fidelity Mechanical Solutions was founded in Houston in 2025 around a simple observation from years in the field: most bad HVAC outcomes aren't caused by bad parts — they're caused by skipped steps.

The FMS Diagnostic Method is the company's answer: a fixed sequence, developed by founder Martin Pérez, that every service call follows in order and without exception. It costs a little more patience on the front end and far less money on the back end, because a system diagnosed correctly once doesn't get repaired twice.

FMS is licensed and insured in the State of Texas and serves homes and businesses across greater Houston, seven days a week.
