The FMS Diagnostic Method

Logic Over Guesswork

A repeatable 6-step sequence that prevents callbacks and ensures nothing is missed. Every technician uses it. Every visit follows it.

Power → Airflow → Electrical → Refrigerant → Controls → Environment
1

Power Verification

Confirm voltage at source and load. Verify disconnects, fuses, breaker behavior. Confirm, don't assume.

2

Airflow Assessment

Filter condition, blower response, static pressure. Never skip airflow and jump to refrigerant.

3

Electrical Systems

Capacitance, contactor integrity, wiring condition. Test coils, relays, amp draw under load.

4

Refrigerant Analysis

Only after airflow is confirmed. Measure superheat and subcool — don't just look at pressure.

5

Controls Verification

Thermostat logic, communication voltage, sensor data, staging, and interlocks verified.

6

Environmental Factors

Rooftop loading, ambient interference, drainage slope, humidity. Site-specific factors matter.
