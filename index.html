The FMS Method

Our Diagnostic Process

A systematic approach that ensures nothing is missed and everything is verified.

1

Power Verification

We confirm voltage at source and load, verify disconnects and breaker behavior. Never test a system that isn't properly powered.

2

Airflow Assessment

Filter condition, blower response, static pressure measurement. Proper airflow is essential before moving to refrigerant diagnostics.

3

Electrical Testing

Capacitance verification, contactor integrity, amp draw under load. Visual inspection of terminals and all electrical connections.

4

Refrigerant Analysis

Superheat and subcool measurements—not just pressure readings. We verify system charge and identify TXV behavior patterns.

5

Controls & Environment

Thermostat logic verification, sensor validation, and environmental assessment. Site-specific factors that affect system performance.

6

Final Verification

System operational under load, all readings documented, client educated on findings. Every service includes a complete verification sweep.
